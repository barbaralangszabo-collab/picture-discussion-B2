How to use this app

Here's what you'll find: it offers 15 B2-level Picture Discussion topics. Each one comes with three images, two guiding questions, a one-minute speaking timer, and ready phrase banks for comparing, contrasting, speculating and hesitating — along with example sentences tailored to whichever topic you choose.

How to use it:

  1. Click Topic and choose one of the 15 topics from the list.
  2. Read the guiding questions and look at the three pictures.
  3. Press Start on the one-minute timer and begin speaking about two of the pictures — use Pause or Reset if you need to.
  4. Click any phrase in Comparing, Contrasting, Speculating or Hesitation Devices to see it used in a full example sentence under Context Examples.
  5. Change topic any time to practise with new pictures and vocabulary.
